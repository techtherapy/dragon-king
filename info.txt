📿 《佛說海龍王經》學習心得
*A Study Reflection on the Sutra Spoken by the Buddha on the Sea Dragon King [Sāgara-nāgarāja-paripṛcchā Sūtra]*

2026.08.08

在浩瀚的佛典中，有一部鮮少人注意卻極為殊勝的經典——《佛說海龍王經》。記載了釋迦牟尼佛在靈鷲山為海龍王及眷屬宣說大乘深義，並應邀至龍宮弘法的莊嚴場景。
*Among the vast collection of Buddhist scriptures there is a text seldom noticed yet exceedingly sublime — the Sutra Spoken by the Buddha on the Sea Dragon King. It records the solemn scene in which Shakyamuni Buddha, upon Vulture Peak, expounded the profound meaning of the Mahayana for the Sea Dragon King and his retinue, and accepted their invitation to propagate the Dharma in the Dragon Palace.*

🐉 一、龍族的來歷
*I. The Origins of the Dragon Clan*

• 龍王不僅是護法神祇，更是能成佛的眾生。他們以前都曾是修行人，因前世犯了戒律才轉世為龍。
*• Dragon kings are not only Dharma Protector deities, but sentient beings capable of attaining Buddhahood. All of them were cultivators in former times, and it was only because they transgressed the precepts in past lives that they were reborn as dragons.*

• 龍族的境遇並不一致，有些龍活得很好，有些卻受苦，甚至被人類欺負。龍並非單純的動物，而是一個種族，就像人間的不同民族一樣。他們同樣在六道輪迴之中，既可能墮入地獄，也可能升上天界。
*• The circumstances of the dragon clan are by no means uniform: some dragons live well, while others suffer, and some are even bullied by humans. Dragons are not simply animals, but a race unto themselves, just as the human world has its different peoples. They too are within the Six Realms of Reincarnation, and may either fall into the hells or ascend to the heavens.*

🔥 二、三熱苦
*II. The Three Burning Sufferings*

• 龍族壽命長、身形大小不一，但也有三種痛苦：
*• Dragons are long-lived and vary in bodily size, yet they also endure three kinds of suffering:*

▪︎ 天上降下灼熱的沙，燙入骨髓。
*▪︎ Scorching sand descends from the sky, scalding into the very marrow of their bones.*

▪︎ 在行成人之事時，忽然被風吹變成蛇，感到羞辱。
*▪︎ While engaging in intimate relations, they are suddenly blown by the wind into the form of snakes, and feel deeply humiliated.*

▪︎ 常憂懼被大鵬金翅鳥抓捕吞食。
*▪︎ They live in constant dread of being seized and devoured by the Garudas.*

🙏 三、師佛的提醒
*III. A Reminder from the Guru Buddha*

• 《龍王經》能帶來最大福報，因為天龍八部最接近人間，祈求必有感應。
*• The Dragon King Sutra brings the greatest merit, because the Eight Classes of Heavenly and Dragon Beings are the closest to the human realm, and prayers made to them will certainly elicit a spiritual response.*

• 誦此經，四部（天部、龍部、阿修羅部、大鵬金翅鳥部）皆會護持，賜予福報。曾有弟子印行此經，很快有感應，得到生活上的利益。
*• Upon reciting this sutra, all four classes — the Deva class, the Dragon class, the Asura class, and the Garuda class — will offer protection and bestow merit. There was once a disciple who printed and circulated this sutra and swiftly received a spiritual response, obtaining benefits in daily life.*

• 師佛讚歎：「《龍王經》是真的很好！因為它調和天龍八部！」
*• The Guru Buddha praised it, saying: "The Dragon King Sutra is truly excellent! Because it harmonizes the Eight Classes of Heavenly and Dragon Beings!"*

📖 四、譯經特色
*IV. Characteristics of the Translation*

• 《龍王經》由竺法護大師所譯，屬於早期譯經作品。當時佛教漢語尚在建立階段，許多名相和用詞還未成熟，因此文字顯得古樸，與現代佛法用語有差異。
*• The Dragon King Sutra was translated by Master Zhu Fahu [Dharmarakṣa] and belongs to the early period of sutra translation. At that time Buddhist Chinese was still in its formative stage; many technical terms and expressions had not yet matured, so the language appears archaic and differs from modern Buddhist usage.*

• 理解這部經典需要注意它的時代背景，不能以後期成熟的譯經標準來衡量。
*• Understanding this sutra requires attention to its historical context; it cannot be measured by the standards of the mature translations of later periods.*

• 突顯佛法在漢地弘揚的過程「早期摸索到後期完善」，逐步形成完整的佛教漢語系統。
*• It highlights the course of the Dharma's propagation in the Chinese lands — "from early exploration to later perfection" — gradually forming a complete system of Buddhist Chinese.*

💡 總結：誦持此《佛說海龍王經》不僅能得天龍八部的護持，福報增長，障礙消除，更能讓行者了解明心見性的真諦。
*💡 Summary: Reciting and upholding this Sutra Spoken by the Buddha on the Sea Dragon King not only secures the protection of the Eight Classes of Heavenly and Dragon Beings, increases merit, and eliminates obstacles, but further enables the cultivator to understand the true meaning of realizing the mind and seeing one's true nature.*

🙏 感恩根本傳承大加持，學習一切吉祥如意圓滿。
*🙏 With infinite gratitude for the great blessings of the Root Lineage; may all our studies be auspicious, fulfilling, and perfectly complete.*
