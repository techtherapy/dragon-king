Take Refuge皈依真佛宗

Taking refuge is a commitment to spiritual cultivation — an aspiration to live a nobler and more meaningful life. Through refuge in the root guru, His Holiness Living Buddha Lian Sheng, one receives the lineage empowerment that opens the gate of Vajrayana practice.

皈依

What refuge means

In the True Buddha School, taking refuge means entrusting oneself to the Triple Jewels — the Buddha, the Dharma and the Sangha — through the root guru who embodies them. It is the doorway to the school's practices, from the Fourfold Refuge onward, and the beginning of an actual path of cultivation rather than admiration from afar.

Refuge is open to everyone. It is conferred on a donation basis with no set fee, so that no one is ever turned away.

儀軌

The simple refuge rite

For refuge at a distance, perform this brief ceremony:

  1. Choose the day. At 7:00 a.m. local time on the first or fifteenth day of any lunar month.
  2. Face the rising sun. With palms joined, recite the Fourfold Refuge mantra three times: “Namo Gurubhya, Namo Buddhaya, Namo Dharmaya, Namo Sanghaya. Seeking the guidance of Living Buddha Lian Sheng, I take refuge in the True Buddha.”
  3. Prostrate three times. The rite itself is complete — then register by one of the methods below, and His Holiness will perform the remote empowerment.
